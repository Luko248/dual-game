Privacy Policy

DUAL: One Mind · Last updated 19 August 2026

DUAL: One Mind collects no personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It makes no network requests of any kind — the game runs entirely on your device, offline.

What the app stores

The game saves a small amount of information so it can remember your progress between sessions. All of it is written to your device's local storage and never leaves your device:

  • Your high score and highest level reached
  • A display name, only if you choose to enter one for the local score board
  • A randomly generated identifier used to keep one score board row per device
  • Your settings: control mode, sound on/off, haptics on/off

None of this is transmitted, shared, or sold. Deleting the app removes all of it.

The leaderboard

The score board in this version of the app is local to your device. It shows only scores achieved on this device and is not synchronised with any server.

Data we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or contact information
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers used for advertising (no IDFA)
  • No usage or diagnostic analytics
  • No photos, contacts, microphone, or camera access — the app requests no permissions

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not share data with anyone. No data is used to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds a feature that transmits data — for example an online leaderboard — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy details will be updated to match.
